Twelve civilians, twelve decks

A build for every character

One six card deck per civilian, each one built around what that character's unique trait does.

Every deck here is six cards. Your deck is a pool that in match perk rolls draw from, so each extra card is one more thing that can come up instead of what you wanted. Six cards you always want means every roll lands. The game caps deck size and does not require you to fill it.

Thomas Armitage

Bully

A college athlete home for the weekend to visit his father. Natural leader, used to organising people under pressure.

Get in Michael's way and stay there. Contact strips your own Stalk, so the thing that would normally kill you is the thing that keeps you safe.

Unique trait
Running Back FLAT
Any contact or physical hit you land on Michael drops your own Stalk tier. Landing a hit is what makes you harder to grab.

Why this character

Fighting Michael has one problem. Standing near him builds Stalk on you passively, Killer Sense takes you to Stalk 3, and the grab prompt is gated on Stalk level. The usual answer is to leave.

Running Back takes Stalk off you on contact. It reads as covering physical contact generally rather than weapon hits alone, so shoulder checks appear to count. Every hit you land undoes his setup on you, and stalking is also his largest source of Bloodthirst, so you tax his power meter at the same time.

It is a flat mechanic rather than a percentage, so card rarity cannot dilute it and an upgrade cannot improve it.

Progressive challenges that involve Michael directly: 7 of 15 (break chase 0, damage 2, down 2, stun 1, grab escape 1, rescue from grab 1).

Loadout

Starting weapon Sledgehammer or Baseball Bat, starting item Pocket Knife. Blunt and heavy. His challenge tree talks about knocking Michael off his feet, and force is what does that. The Pocket Knife is the one grab breaker that gets you out instantly rather than shortening the struggle.

How to play it

  1. Find him on a Resident, because that is his actual objective and that is where he will be.
  2. Make contact early. With Running Back the approach is the safe part of the exchange.
  3. Trade and move. Stay inside his Shape Jump prevention radius so he cannot blink away.
  4. Break chase on purpose when your stamina bottoms out. Break Chase is scored at three separate tiers.
  5. Hold a flashlight on him whenever somebody else is swinging, because light drains Bloodthirst and fills the detainment meter.

Bob Simms

Chase tank

Lynda's boyfriend. Quiet, along for the ride, and the one who went for the beer.

The most anti-Michael character in the game by the numbers, and the forgiving version of the bully.

Unique trait
Heavyweight FLAT
You get the upside of being drunk with none of the downside. No stumbling, no extra noise, less visual impairment, plus reduced fear and a melee boost.

Why this character

Eleven of Bob's fifteen progressive challenges involve Michael directly, more than anybody else on the roster. He is one of three civilians whose tree covers all three Stalk marker tiers, and the only one that pairs that with four combat challenges.

Heavyweight removes the downsides of drinking and keeps the melee boost and the fear reduction. Beer is common loot, described in game as something that helps keep you calm.

The fear half matters more than it looks. Past a threshold, fear stops you using a phone and stops you holding a conversation outright rather than just making them worse. Police pressure comes from phone calls, so a bully who can keep calling while scared is worth more to the team.

Progressive challenges that involve Michael directly: 11 of 15 (break chase 3, damage 2, down 2, stun 0, grab escape 2, rescue from grab 1).

Stealth rescuer

Spatial Awareness highlights Residents up to 25 metres away, and it only works when you are calm. Fear control is a direct trait enabler for her in a way it is not for anybody else.

Her challenge tree is stealth and rescues, and it also carries all three Stalk marker tiers alongside real combat, which puts her third on the anti-Michael list without ever asking her to start the fight.

Residents are the win condition. A character who can see them through walls while staying calm is doing the objective and the scouting at once.

Rachel Calahan

Bloodthirst denial

Proto goth with an occult streak. Cemeteries over bowling alleys, and proud of the weirdo label.

Every hit you land takes more off his power meter than anyone else's does. Build around downing him.

Damaging and downing Michael takes more off his Bloodthirst than it would for anybody else.

Why this character

Full Moon means her damage and her knockdowns take more off his Bloodthirst than anybody else's do. Bloodthirst is his global power meter, and it drives melee damage, Shape speed, cooldowns and access to his Bloodthirst executions.

That makes Rachel the one who should land the finishing hit, and it makes her the best partner for a detainment plan.

Her challenge tree is fear immunity all the way down, with three Stalk marker tiers and two grab escapes on top.

Progressive challenges that involve Michael directly: 6 of 15 (break chase 3, damage 0, down 0, stun 1, grab escape 2, rescue from grab 0).

Evacuator

Haddonfield's it girl, back from a failed run at big city modelling, working at Strode Realty.

The best follower build in the game. Take more Residents, keep them alive, get them out.

Helping Residents escape is the civilian objective. Alexis does the objective faster than anyone.

Dreamer
Residents you successfully talk to become harder for Michael to stalk, and it sticks after you walk away.

Why this character

Dreamer makes Residents you successfully talk to harder for Michael to stalk, and the protection travels with them after you have moved on.

Her challenge tree is routes and planning: finding a way out, planning ahead, handling the moment it goes wrong.

She is the worst pick on the roster for fighting Michael. Three of her fifteen challenges involve him at all and none involve hurting him. Playing her as a brawler is playing against the design.

Progressive challenges that involve Michael directly: 3 of 15 (break chase 1, damage 0, down 0, stun 0, grab escape 1, rescue from grab 0).

How to play it

  1. Open routes before the team needs them. An escape discovered under pressure is usually too late.
  2. Talk to every Resident you pass. Dreamer is applied by the interaction, so it costs you nothing extra.
  3. Mark what you find for the team immediately.
  4. Leave the fighting alone. You are the map, and the map should not be in the fight.

Richard Hawthorne

Party brawler

New money from California, crowned party king within a month of arriving.

Get drunk, get high, hit harder than anybody, and accept that you will walk into a wall doing it.

Unique trait
Affluent Partier
Holding both the drunk and the high states at once adds a further melee bonus on top of whatever each is already giving you.

Why this character

Affluent Partier pays out a further melee bonus once you are holding both the drunk state and the high state together. It is the only trait that asks you to stack two consumables.

Unlike Bob, Richard eats the downsides. Drinking brings movement instability, extra noise and visual impairment, and he has no Heavyweight to strip them out. The trait pays you to accept all of that.

Witness State pays out on the same condition from the other side: while high, other characters within range have faint outlines. Being high is both his damage buff and his information layer.

Progressive challenges that involve Michael directly: 2 of 15 (break chase 2, damage 0, down 0, stun 0, grab escape 0, rescue from grab 0).

Loadout

Starting weapon Sharp Pool Cue or Broken Bottle, starting item Beer. Thematically his, and the beer starts the engine. Find a joint on the map to finish it.

How to play it

  1. Get both states up before you look for him, because half the build is off until you do.
  2. Fight near your team. Your vision is impaired and theirs is not.
  3. Use Witness State as a scouting tool between fights rather than only in them.
  4. Re dose constantly. Reveler helps, but neither state lasts forever.

Reading the ordering

The builds are ordered by how much each character's own progressive challenge tree points them at Michael. Each civilian has fifteen challenges, and we counted the ones that need you to act against him: downing him, damaging him, stunning him, escaping a grab, pulling somebody else out of a grab, breaking chase, or baiting him. That measures what the character was designed to do and says nothing about raw power. Bob leads it and Tanya sits last, which does not make Tanya weak. It makes her a scout.
