A perk deck for every civilian

Twelve six card decks, each built around what that character’s trait does. No invented perks, and no percentages that nobody can source.

What drives every build

01

His objective is the Residents, not you

Michael is scored on killing the NPC townsfolk, and the game lists killing the four player civilians as a separate objective marked optional. Yours is to get Residents out. Every second he spends chasing you is a second off his own win condition, which is why getting in his way is a real plan rather than a stunt.

02

A deck is a pool, not a loadout

During a match you earn perk rolls, and each one draws a card out of the deck you brought. Every extra card is one more thing that can come up instead of what you wanted. Six cards you always want beats fifteen where six are good.

03

One unique trait each

Twelve traits for twelve civilians. Everything else a guide calls a trait is an ordinary deck perk the character happens to start with, and none of the twelve trait names appears among the 62 deck perks.

04

You can arrest him

Detaining Michael ends the match on the spot. Police near him fill a detainment meter, flashlights fill it faster, and once it is full a knockdown opens a long hold interaction. That makes police perks, lights and Knockout worth more than they look.

About this site

The builds come from reading what each perk and trait does and working out which levers matter, rather than from copying a tier list. Every description here is written for this site in plain terms. Nothing is copied from the game, and no game files, assets or data exports are hosted or distributed.

What you will not find here is numbers. The game keeps the actual percentages behind each effect separately from the effect itself, so the honest position is that the levers are known and their sizes are not. If a card turns out to be worth three percent, its place in a deck moves, and this site would be wrong about the ordering.

Civilian stats are absent for the same reason. The four attributes are Capability, Athleticism, Personality and Resourcefulness, and the numbers behind them are not something we can stand behind. The stat tables circulating online have no source, so repeating them here would only launder a guess.

Every effect description on this site is written here. None of it is copied from the game, and no game files, assets or data exports are distributed. Percentages are deliberately absent: the effects are known, the magnitudes are not, and nothing on this site invents one.
